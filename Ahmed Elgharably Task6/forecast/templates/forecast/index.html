Two years of daily NASA POWER records · 31.42°N, 31.81°E

What will the air over Damietta feel like?

This forecasts daily mean temperature from a SARIMAX(2,1,2) model fit with three harmonics of an annual Fourier cycle as exogenous regressors — the approach Task 5 landed on after finding a literal 365-day seasonal order intractable. Two honest baselines, persistence and seasonal-naive, sit alongside it, because Task 5's own evaluation found persistence actually wins at long, static horizons on this smooth, coastal series.

Humidity, wind and pressure aren't in the form below — Task 4's EDA found them only weakly correlated with temperature here (r ≈ −0.1), so they wouldn't move a temperature forecast in any meaningful way.
