About SARIMA

SARIMA (Seasonal AutoRegressive Integrated Moving Average) is a time series forecasting method that:

  • Captures seasonality: Identifies and models repeating patterns (e.g., weekly cycles)
  • Handles trends: Uses differencing to make non-stationary data stationary
  • Uses autocorrelation: Leverages past observations to predict future values
  • Provides uncertainty: Generates confidence intervals around predictions
How Predictions Work
  1. The model analyzes the complete historical temperature time series
  2. It identifies seasonal patterns (7-day cycles in this case)
  3. It applies differencing to remove trends
  4. It uses ARIMA components to model autoregressive and moving average effects
  5. Based on these patterns, it forecasts temperature for your requested horizon
  6. It also provides a 95% confidence interval around the prediction

Limitations
  • Predictions are based on historical patterns and may not account for unprecedented weather events
  • The model was trained on {{ weather_location.name }}-specific data; apply with caution to other locations
  • Forecasts are less reliable for horizons beyond 4 weeks
  • Confidence intervals assume the past patterns will continue into the future
  • This is a statistical model, not a weather simulation; it does not include physical weather processes
